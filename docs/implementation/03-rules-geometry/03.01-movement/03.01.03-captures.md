Apply immediate deterministic captures while preserving references required by settlement and history systems.

## Acceptance criteria

- Captured pieces leave occupancy exactly once and generate a typed transition event.
- Capturing a founder or settlement-produced Pawn preserves enough identity for realm cleanup.
- Kings are never directly captured; positions ending in an attacked, inescapable King resolve as checkmate.
- Capture application and rollback-free validation are tested.
