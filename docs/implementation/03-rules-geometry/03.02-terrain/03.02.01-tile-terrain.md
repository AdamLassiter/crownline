# Task 03.02.01: Implement tile terrain

Apply forest and mountain behavior to step, jump, and sliding movement.

## Dependencies

- 03.01.02.

## Acceptance criteria

- A slider may enter the first forest square but may not attack or move beyond it.
- Mountains reject all landings; Knights may jump across them.
- Forest occupancy and piece blockers interact consistently.
- Road tiles have no rules effect.

## Cross-cutting concerns

- Terrain explanations identify the blocking coordinate and terrain kind.
