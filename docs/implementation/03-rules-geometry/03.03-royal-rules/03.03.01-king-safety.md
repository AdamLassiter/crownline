# Task 03.03.01: Filter moves for King safety

Simulate pseudo-legal actions against canonical state and retain only actions leaving the acting King unattacked.

## Dependencies

- 03.01.03, 03.02.03.

## Acceptance criteria

- Pinned pieces cannot expose their King.
- King destinations attacked geometrically by an opponent are rejected.
- Checks can be escaped only by a King move, attacker capture, or valid interposition.
- Checkmate is reported when no legal Move exists and the King is checked.

## Cross-cutting concerns

- Property tests assert that every generated legal Move preserves royal safety.
