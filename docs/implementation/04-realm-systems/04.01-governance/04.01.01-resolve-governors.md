# Task 04.01.01: Resolve governors

Resolve all friendly major pieces geometrically covering each owned settlement and report their lines and blockers.

## Dependencies

- 03.02.03, 03.03.01.

## Acceptance criteria

- A friendly founder occupying the settlement endpoint does not prevent governance.
- Intervening pieces, forests, mountains, rivers, and walls block governance exactly as their attack geometry dictates.
- Pinned pieces still govern geometrically even when moving would be illegal.
- Multiple governors are returned in stable order.

## Cross-cutting concerns

- The resolver supports both rules evaluation and UI explanation.
