# Task 04.01.02: Track continuous governance

Track whether each settlement met founder, ownership, occupancy, and governance requirements throughout its development cycle.

## Status

## Implementation notes

- Every accepted reducer action reevaluates owned settlements from canonical state and latches interruption when the founder is absent/wrong, an enemy occupies the site, or no geometric governor remains.
- The latch is monotonic within a cycle: restoring all requirements later cannot clear an earlier interruption.
- On a non-terminal owner-turn boundary, the completed result is snapshotted to `completed_cycle_continuous` and the live latch resets for the new cycle; development/production tasks consume that snapshot.
- Typed transition events report both the first interruption and each owner-cycle boundary with its completed continuity result.
- Both live and completed continuity fields are canonical, hashed, saved, and therefore journal/replay deterministic. Focused tests cover action-time interruption, restored-but-still-failed completion, reset, events, and save/load.

## Acceptance criteria

- Any accepted action that breaks a requirement marks that cycle interrupted.
- Restoring governance later does not retroactively repair the interrupted cycle.
- A successful owner-turn advancement resets continuity tracking for the next cycle.
- Save/load and replay preserve identical continuity state.

## Cross-cutting concerns

- Track state transitions, not frame-by-frame observations.
