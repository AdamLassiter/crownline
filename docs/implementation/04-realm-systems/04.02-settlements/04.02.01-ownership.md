# Task 04.02.01: Implement settlement claiming and transfer

Claim neutral settlements on a Pawn landing and transfer hostile settlements only after the occupying Pawn survives until its next owner turn.

## Dependencies

- 04.01.02.

## Status

- [x] Done

## Implementation notes

- A Pawn Move ending on a neutral settlement claims it in the same deterministic transition, retaining the Pawn as the stable founder identity.
- An enemy Pawn landing creates a transfer candidate and immediately interrupts continuity without changing ownership.
- Candidate identity and site occupancy are revalidated after every accepted action; capture or departure cancels the transfer with a typed event.
- At the candidate owner's next non-terminal turn boundary, transfer resolves before continuity completion, installs the surviving Pawn as founder, and forces the completed cycle ineligible so ownership never grants free advancement.
- Design decision: transfer preserves establishment/production progress and any still-living supported Pawn; conquest changes control rather than destroying infrastructure. Continuity still gates subsequent advancement.
- Claim, contest, cancellation, and transfer all emit stable-identity domain events for journals, networking, and UI.

## Acceptance criteria

- Claim records the Pawn as founder without removing it.
- Enemy occupancy immediately blocks development but does not immediately transfer ownership.
- Capturing or leaving before the survival boundary cancels transfer.
- Successful transfer installs the surviving Pawn as the new founder and starts with no free development advancement.

## Cross-cutting concerns

- Ownership changes are explicit transition events for UI and journals.
