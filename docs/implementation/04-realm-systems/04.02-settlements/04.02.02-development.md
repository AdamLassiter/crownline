## Implementation notes

- Owner-turn cycle completion now consumes the latched continuity result after transfer resolution and before resetting the next cycle.
- An unestablished settlement advances exactly one bounded step only when founder identity, ownership, enemy occupancy, and geometric governance remained valid for the complete cycle.
- Progress clamps to the validated scenario threshold and emits typed advancement and establishment events; reaching the configured threshold establishes the settlement.
- Interrupted cycles pause under the shipped policy. The existing scenario option can explicitly reset progress instead, with a distinct transition event.
- Establishment is persistent: later interruption or founder movement cannot reduce progress or unset the established flag.
- Focused tests cover configured thresholds, establishment events, shipped pause behavior, optional reset behavior, and persistence after founder movement.

## Acceptance criteria

- Founder presence, friendly ownership, no enemy occupation, and continuous governance are all required.
- Interrupted development pauses at its current value under shipped scenario rules.
- Establishment persists after later interruption or founder movement.
- Thresholds come from validated scenario configuration.
