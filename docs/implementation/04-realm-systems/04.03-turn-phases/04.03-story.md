# Story 04.03: Promotion and turn phases

As a player, I need start-of-turn processes and choices to resolve in a consistent, understandable order.

## Acceptance criteria

- Surviving candidate Pawns promote to a selected Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.
- Simultaneous automatic effects and choices resolve in stable coordinate order.
- Each completed turn contains exactly one Move or Hold.

## Cross-cutting concerns

- Pending phases serialize, reconnect, replay, and display without ambiguity.
