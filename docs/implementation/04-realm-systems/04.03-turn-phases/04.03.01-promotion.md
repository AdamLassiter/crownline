# Task 04.03.01: Implement promotion candidates

Mark Pawns ending a Move on promotion sites and queue promotion if they survive until their next owner turn.

## Dependencies

- 03.03.01, 02.02.02.

## Status

- [x] Done

## Implementation notes

- A Pawn Move ending on a fixed promotion site creates a canonical candidate at zero progress; configured owner-turn survival cycles advance only while the same Pawn remains on a site.
- Departure or capture removes candidacy immediately and emits a typed cancellation event. Surviving candidates queue mandatory choices at their owner's boundary.
- The closed `PromotionKind` enum permits only Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight. Selection retires the Pawn identity, allocates a new stable promoted identity, and preserves owner and coordinate.
- Promotion is transactionally rejected if the resulting canonical position leaves the active King in check.
- Promotion clears founder, transfer, candidacy, and settlement-produced-Pawn references, reopening production capacity when applicable.
- Candidate start/progress/cancellation/readiness and final replacement are serializable domain events; focused tests cover survival, cancellation by departure/capture, identity replacement, capacity release, and King-safety rejection.

## Acceptance criteria

- Leaving or being captured before the boundary cancels candidacy.
- Replacement preserves coordinate and player while retiring the Pawn identity.
- Choices are limited to Queen, Rook, Bishop, and Knight.
- The resulting position passes King-safety validation before commitment.

## Cross-cutting concerns

- Promotion releases any settlement production capacity linked to the Pawn.
