# Task 04.03.02: Implement turn-start resolution

Resolve survival boundaries, transfers, eligible development/production progress, and mandatory choices at each owner-turn start.

## Implementation notes

- Owner-turn boundaries resolve surviving transfer candidates before evaluating settlement development, so a settlement acquired at that boundary cannot gain immediate progress.
- Development, production, and promotion eligibility use the continuity snapshot completed by the preceding owner cycle. Mandatory choices made afterward only interrupt the new cycle.
- Ready Pawn placements and promotions share one mandatory-choice queue sorted by board coordinate and then choice type, independent of insertion order.
- Turn-start effects emit typed, serializable events for transfer, progress, readiness, and choice changes; focused tests compare complete transitions and replay the sequence from an action journal without time input.

## Acceptance criteria

- Transfer candidates resolve before development eligibility, and newly transferred settlements do not advance immediately.
- Eligibility uses continuity from the completed cycle, not changes caused by current choices.
- Promotion choices and Pawn placements queue in stable coordinate and type order.
- Turn-start transitions are replayable without reading a clock.
