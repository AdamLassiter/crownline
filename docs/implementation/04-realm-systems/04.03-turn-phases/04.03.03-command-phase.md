# Task 04.03.03: Implement Move and Hold phases

Allow one legal Move or Hold after mandatory choices and advance to the opponent's turn.

## Status

- [x] Done

## Implementation notes

- Move and Hold use the same transactional reducer boundary, require the active player in the command phase, complete exactly one turn, and advance to the opponent.
- Pending mandatory choices and terminal outcomes reject both commands without changing the source state. Hold is additionally rejected while the active King is checked.
- Hold clears transient en-passant state but preserves piece occupancy, emits a typed `TurnHeld` event, increments the revision, and is persisted as an explicit journal action.
- Realm continuity is latched after every accepted action and completed owner-cycle snapshots are recomputed at the opponent boundary.
- A focused stalemate fixture proves that a player with no legal Move but not in check can Hold and replay that action, so checkmate remains the only no-move terminal chess condition.
- Player-facing Hold availability and explanation remain assigned to Tasks 07.01.01 and 07.02.02.

## Dependencies

- 04.03.02.

## Acceptance criteria

- Move/Hold is rejected while choices remain or after match termination.
- Hold is rejected while the active King is checked.
- Hold changes no board occupancy but completes the turn and records a journal action.
- Realm continuity is recomputed after the accepted command.

## Cross-cutting concerns

- Hold makes non-check stalemate impossible; the UI must explain its availability.
