# Task 04.04.02: Implement draws, resignation, and outcomes

Resolve checkmate, timeout, resignation, accepted draws, and automatic threefold full-state repetition.

## Status

- [x] Done

## Implementation notes

- Checkmate, timeout, resignation, agreed draw, and threefold repetition produce stable `OutcomeReason` values and one typed match-ended event.
- A single outstanding draw offer may be explicitly accepted or rejected. Any accepted opponent Move, Hold, promotion, or Pawn-placement action implicitly rejects it and emits a structured negative draw answer.
- Repetition hashes active player, phase and ordered choices, pieces and lineage, en-passant and castling rights, settlement lifecycle state, and promotion candidates while excluding clocks, revision/turn metadata, and presentation state.
- Focused fixtures prove automatic termination on the third full-state occurrence and distinguish otherwise similar states across realm phases, rights, choices, and lineage.
- All gameplay actions are rejected before dispatch once a terminal outcome exists; rematch negotiation remains outside canonical match actions.
- Receipt-time race tests prove an exact-deadline timeout prevents a submitted checkmating move, while the same move received one millisecond earlier wins by checkmate and makes later timeout processing inert.

## Acceptance criteria

- Repetition includes pieces, side/phase, special rights, settlements, candidates, lineage, and choices but excludes clocks and UI state.
- One draw offer may be outstanding; acceptance terminates, rejection clears, and an opponent's normal turn action implicitly rejects it.
- Terminal states reject gameplay actions but allow rematch negotiation externally.
- Outcome tests cover ordering races between commands, checkmate, and timeout.

## Cross-cutting concerns

- Outcome reasons are stable protocol values, not localized display strings.
