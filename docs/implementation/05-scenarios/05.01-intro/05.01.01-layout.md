# Task 05.01.01: Author the 16x16 layout

Create a rotationally symmetric RON scenario with simplified Keeps and short routes to contested objectives.

## Dependencies

- 02.01.02, 04.04.02.

## Acceptance criteria

- Both armies deploy legally with at least two Keep exits.
- Settlements present one safe-side and one contested-side decision per player.
- Promotion sites are exposed and reachable by multiple routes.
- Optional double-step, en passant, and designated castling are enabled.

## Cross-cutting concerns

- Roads clarify likely lines but confer no rules bonus.
