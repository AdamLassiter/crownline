## Implementation notes

- Added `assets/scenarios/large.ron` as the data-only, 24x24 "The Three Theatres" scenario with exact 180-degree rotational symmetry and a 105-135 minute target.
- Paired vertical rivers divide western, central, and eastern theatres. Each river has two bridges and two fords at separated ranks, so no piece can seal an entire crossing region and every front has multiple connections.
- Six settlements distribute objectives across all three theatres. Four promotion courts occupy flank and inner approaches, each with at least two orthogonally open entry squares.
- Sparse forest pockets and crossing-side crags create Knight bypasses and slider tradeoffs while leaving long central files and diagonals open for attack and governance networks.
- Each full standard army has four Keep exits, paired tower projections, and two validated castling routes; road tinting links Keep lanes to early branching points.
- Authored-layout tests parse and validate the RON, verify complete counts and duration, enforce four crossings per river, check promotion approach multiplicity, and prove rotational equivalence for terrain, edges, armies, settlements, and promotion sites.

## Acceptance criteria

- Multiple routes connect every theatre; no side can seal all crossings permanently with one piece.
- Long files and diagonals create valuable governance and attack tradeoffs.
- Promotion sites remain contestable from more than one approach.
- Full-army deployments and castling routes validate.
