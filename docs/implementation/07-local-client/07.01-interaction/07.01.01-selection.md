# Task 07.01.01: Implement selection and confirmation

Implement select/reselect/cancel and destination confirmation for Move, plus an explicit Hold control.

## Dependencies

- 06.02.01, 06.03.01, 04.03.03.

## Status

- [x] Done

## Implementation notes

- Mouse clicks and keyboard Enter share one activation function: an active piece selects or reselects, a reducer-reported destination submits Move, and every other square clears selection.
- Accepted commands replace canonical state immediately and enqueue the reducer's ordered transition events. A per-submission guard and edge-triggered input prevent a single activation from being submitted twice; presentation animations do not participate in this boundary.
- Selection is presentation-only and is cleared whenever an externally changed canonical revision is observed. Overlay destinations continue to come from `legal_moves`, so the interaction layer does not duplicate movement rules.
- Arrow keys establish and move a visible board-focus square within board bounds. Enter activates it and Escape releases board focus and selection, preventing keyboard focus from becoming trapped.
- The always-visible interaction text exposes the Hold shortcut and its current state. Hold is rejected before submission with a specific explanation while the King is checked, a mandatory choice is pending, or the match is terminal.
- Tests cover select/reselect/cancel/legal-confirm behavior, exactly one revision advance for a submitted Move, disabled Hold explanations, and keyboard-focus boundary clamping.

## Acceptance criteria

- Selecting an active piece shows only its legal destinations.
- Clicking another active piece reselects; clicking elsewhere cancels unless it is a legal destination.
- A destination click submits exactly once.
- Hold is disabled with an explanation while checked or awaiting choices.

## Cross-cutting concerns

- Keyboard focus remains visible and never becomes trapped on the board.
