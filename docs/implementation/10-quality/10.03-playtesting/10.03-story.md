# Story 10.03: Playtesting and accessibility

As the design team, we need evidence that Crownlines is understandable, conflict-rich, and playable by its intended audience.

## Status

- [ ] In progress

## Implementation notes

- Task 10.03.00 provides explicit name-free local playtest export with canonical action/event/hash evidence, all required balance metrics, qualitative review fields, and a side-swapped/tuning protocol; it adds no telemetry or automatic upload.
- Task 10.03.03 adds archived, deterministic West/East opening probes for every shipped scenario, proving bounded central crossings, objective interaction, and absence of immediate first-move checkmate without treating automation as human balance evidence.
- Task 10.03.02 completes the accessibility audit with non-hue semantic marks and text, keyboard-only controls with visible focus/selection, bounded configured scaling across common resolutions, and reduced-motion feedback invariants.
- Structured human balance records remain in Task 10.03.01.
