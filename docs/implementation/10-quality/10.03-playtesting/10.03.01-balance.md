# Task 10.03.01: Run structured balance playtests

Use mirrored openings and full matches across all scenarios to measure contact timing, development, promotions, checks, and outcomes.

## Dependencies

- Epic 05, 07.03, 09.03.

## Acceptance criteria

- Records include scenario/version, turn count, duration, first contact, claims, produced/promoted Pawns, checks, and outcome.
- Review explicitly addresses governance clarity, growth speed, economic conflict, promotion pressure, downtime, major-piece overload, and checkmate viability.
- First-player and geographic bias are evaluated with side-swapped games.
- Every tuning change states hypothesis and follow-up result.

## Cross-cutting concerns

- No network telemetry is added; playtest export is explicit and local.
