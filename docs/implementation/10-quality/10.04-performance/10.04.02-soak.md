## Implementation notes

- Added an ignored release-profile soak that drives eight clocked rooms through the real loopback HTTP/WebSocket server and file-backed SQLite WAL persistence. It covers join/readiness, accepted actions, invalid authentication, malformed input, disconnect/reconnect, active and resigned rooms, and deterministic cleanup.
- Held one authenticated subscriber unread across 40 draw offer/reject revisions, exceeding the 32-event broadcast window, while publishers continued. The subscriber subsequently reached the exact latest authoritative snapshot; snapshot delivery remains a one-value Tokio watch channel rather than an unbounded per-client queue.
- Added live-database query and size accounting, online `VACUUM INTO` backup with integrity checking, full server restart from the backup, reauthentication, and stored-versus-recomputed canonical hash verification.
- Documented the 2026-07-22 local baseline in `docs/soak-baseline.md`: 8 rooms, 59 actions, 67 snapshots, a 978,944-byte backup, 140-microsecond action-count query, 5 ms backup/integrity pass, and RSS of 4,784 KiB before start versus 15,432 KiB after socket cleanup and server stop.
- Added `scripts/soak.sh` and scheduled/manual CI execution after the complete quality matrix. The loopback-only harness creates temporary databases and synthetic identities; it cannot target a deployed server.
