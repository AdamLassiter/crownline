# Task 12.01.04: Render fog and secure local handoff

Present each visibility state accessibly and prevent hot-seat transitions or UI details from exposing the other player's view.

## Status

- [ ] Not started

## Dependencies

- 12.01.03, 06.01, 06.02, 06.03, 07.01, 07.02, 07.03, Bug 017.

## Scope

- Render undiscovered squares with a neutral opaque treatment that exposes no checker parity or terrain tint; render explored squares with permanent terrain/site information but a dim non-current treatment; render visible squares normally.
- Combine luminance, pattern/symbol, and text legend cues so the three states do not rely on hue.
- Spawn enemy piece entities, dynamic settlement details, overlays, hover previews, transition ghosts/notices, and side-panel text only from the current seat projection.
- Remove a non-visible enemy immediately without leaving motion, capture, or last-known-position residue.
- Replace the view with a full-screen handoff curtain as soon as a local turn ends. Require an explicit next-player confirmation before constructing the new seat projection; pause gameplay input and local clocks according to a documented fair-play policy during handoff.
- Rebuild projection state safely after save/load, scenario change, rematch, resize, and orientation swap without a one-frame canonical flash.

## Acceptance criteria

- Pixel and ECS tests distinguish all three square states and prove undiscovered squares share no terrain-dependent presentation data.
- Hidden enemy pieces and dynamic facts are absent, not transparent or placed behind a fog sprite.
- Local turn handoff displays no outgoing or incoming board frame until the next player confirms.
- Keyboard focus, pointer picking, coordinate labels, help, and the Bug 017 screen regions remain usable in every visibility state.
- Reduced motion and font fallback cannot reveal hidden entities or stale glyphs.

## Cross-cutting concerns

- Accessibility, no transient disclosure, projection-only Bevy inputs, hot-seat shoulder-surfing limitations, and revision-cached entity churn.
