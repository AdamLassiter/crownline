## Acceptance criteria

- Automated disclosure tests fail when any seeded hidden fact appears in a forbidden surface.
- Fog-enabled replays and reconnects reproduce both players' exploration masks and projection hashes at every revision.
- Existing perfect-information golden fixtures and UX remain unchanged.
- Radius 3 is retained or changed only with recorded playtest evidence, and the final scenario value is documented as tuning rather than a global rule.
- Players can explain undiscovered/explored/visible states, why an enemy disappeared, which match facts remain public, and when full replay truth becomes available.
- 24x24 fog computation, payload sizes, and rendering remain within recorded release-profile budgets.
