# Task 13.01.03: Add move ordering and quiescence

Reduce obvious horizon mistakes without turning the initial opponent into a complex chess-engine project.

## Status

- [ ] Not started

## Dependencies

- 13.01.02, 03.01.03, 03.03.01, 04.02, 04.03.01.

## Scope

- Order the prior principal-variation action first, then terminal/mating actions, mandatory resolutions, promotions, captures, checks, settlement transfers/claims, and quiet actions with stable coordinate/action tie-breaks.
- At the ordinary horizon, extend only bounded noisy actions: captures, checks, promotions, forced mandatory choices, and immediate settlement ownership/production changes.
- Include a stand-pat evaluation only when the side is not in check and no mandatory choice is pending.
- Cap quiescence depth and nodes independently; include repetition/terminal handling and prevent an unbounded sequence of checks or realm effects.
- Keep ordering and noisy-action classification replaceable and independently testable.

## Acceptance criteria

- Horizon fixtures avoid hanging-piece, immediate recapture, forced-promotion, and one-step settlement-transfer blunders caught by the declared noisy set.
- Quiet positions terminate at stand pat, checked positions search legal evasions, and mandatory choices are always resolved.
- Quiescence respects its depth/node caps and reports its work separately from ordinary nodes.
- Ordering changes node count but never changes minimax correctness at a fully searched fixed depth.

## Cross-cutting concerns

- Explicit scope control, deterministic ordering, bounded tactical extensions, realm-rule coverage, and no cache keyed by unstable iteration order.
