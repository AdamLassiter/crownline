## Acceptance criteria

- Evaluation combines ordinary material with mobility, royal safety, Pawns/promotion, terrain, settlements, governance, development, and production.
- Named difficulties have explicit evaluator features and depth/node/quiescence budgets and remain legal under identical rules.
- Local setup supports Human vs AI on either side plus developer-only AI vs AI without blocking rendering or accepting a stale decision.
- Decision diagnostics can explain score components and search limits without claiming the heuristic is optimal play.

## Cross-cutting concerns

- Explainability, stable tuning data, responsive cancellation, clock accounting, accessibility, and no random difficulty cheating.
