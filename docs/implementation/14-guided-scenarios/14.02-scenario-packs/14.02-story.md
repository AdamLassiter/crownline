## Acceptance criteria

- The tutorial sequence introduces one small set of concepts at a time, recalls earlier concepts, and ends in open practice against AI.
- Challenge puzzles cover both chess tactics and Crownlines-specific trade-offs rather than relabeling ordinary mate puzzles.
- Every exact puzzle has a machine-verified solution/defense tree under canonical rules and every open scenario has reproducible AI smoke evidence.
- Difficulty progression is supported by branching factor/search metrics and human completion/hint/retry evidence.

## Cross-cutting concerns

- No tutorial-only rule exceptions, data-only tuning, accessibility, representative player evidence, deterministic archives, and clear distinction between instruction and challenge.
