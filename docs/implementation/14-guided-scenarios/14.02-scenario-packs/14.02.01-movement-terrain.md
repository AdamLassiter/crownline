## Scope

- Build short staged positions for selection/movement, capture/blocking, Knight jumps, forest ray stopping, mountain blocking, river crossings, and Rook-linked Gates.
- Use minimal valid material and uncluttered boards while retaining normal canonical movement/check rules.
- Explain legal and illegal outcomes after player action, then require an unprompted application before completion.
- Finish with Apprentice AI open practice that requires crossing terrain and making a safe capture rather than following one exact move list.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each terrain/movement mechanic is first demonstrated, then practiced, then assessed without an automatic move hint.
- Illegal attempts explain the relevant blocker without changing state or consuming a turn.
- A player cannot complete the assessment solely by advancing through text.
- Automated runs prove every stage is reachable and the open-practice AI has legal bounded replies.

## Cross-cutting concerns

- Small readable positions, keyboard path, non-color terrain cues, canonical legality, and localization-ready explanations.
