## Scope

- Teach Pawn claim/founder identity, valid major-piece governance, line blockers, continuity across owner turns, establishment, production readiness, Pawn placement, contest, cancellation, and transfer.
- Use staged examples where moving a governor improves material but pauses progress, then ask the player to choose and explain the consequence through UI feedback.
- Include a multi-settlement line showing one Rook governing efficiently and the vulnerability of a shared blocker.
- End with Steward AI open practice whose evaluator includes realm features and whose position admits more than one defensible plan.

## Acceptance criteria

- Players must successfully claim, govern through a complete cycle, establish, produce/place a Pawn, and identify one interruption cause.
- The lesson distinguishes ownership, founder presence, governance, establishment progress, production progress, readiness, and supported Pawn.
- No stage advances from a merely visual overlay when the canonical predicate/event did not occur.
- Automated replays cover the intended path plus blocker, capture, and transfer recovery branches.
