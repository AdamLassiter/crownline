## Scope

- Teach double-step/en-passant windows, scenario promotion candidacy, mandatory promotion choice, check evasions, pins, castling routes, Hold restrictions, checkmate, and draw outcomes.

## Acceptance criteria

- The player executes or correctly responds to every special/royal mechanic without a tutorial-only legality exception.
- Mandatory choices, check, and outcome guidance use canonical phase/events and survive save/resume.
- At least one assessment has multiple legal moves but only a subset meeting the declared lesson objective.
- The final practice match can finish by a canonical outcome and records which reminders/hints were requested.

## Cross-cutting concerns
