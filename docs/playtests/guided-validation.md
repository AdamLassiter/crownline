# Guided scenario validation record

This record separates reproducible implementation evidence from human findings.
It covers the 28 guided scenarios generated by `generate_guided_scenarios` at
guided schema version 1. It does not claim that a player understood, enjoyed,
or correctly ranked the content.

## Reproducible evidence

| Area | Archived or executable evidence | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Construction and content drift | `cargo run -p crownline_core --example generate_guided_scenarios` validates and RON-round-trips every start, then rewrites every checked-in scenario and the exact challenge archive. | 28 valid guided scenarios; competitive scenario documents remain separate. |
| Movement objectives | `cargo test -p crownline_core --test guided_movement_pack` | Every declared solution reaches its objective; illegal blockers are transactional. |
| Realm objectives | `cargo test -p crownline_core --test guided_realm_pack` | Claim, governance, production, transfer, cancellation, and shared-governance paths pass. |
| Royal objectives | `cargo test -p crownline_core --test guided_royal_pack` | En passant, locked/frozen promotion, check, Hold rejection, castling, mate, and draw pass. |
| Exact challenge solver | `cargo test -p crownline_core --test guided_challenge_pack` and `assets/scenarios/guided/challenge-solutions.ron` | All legal first actions are enumerated. Six scenario/start hashes, branching factors, feature tags, and complete accepted solution sets match the archive. |
| AI response | `cargo test -p crownline_ai --test guided_open_practice` | Apprentice, both Steward practices, and Warden repeat byte-equivalent legal decisions within configured bounds. |
| Save/resume and local metrics | `guided_play::tests::resume_round_trip_restores_exact_canonical_state_and_rejects_bad_hint_count` and adjacent progress tests | Exact canonical current/retry state, stage, attempts, retries, hints, and best action/turn counts round-trip atomically. |
| Replay/objective parity | `guided::tests::objective_result_matches_live_save_and_replay_states` | Live, restored, and replayed predicates agree. |
| Input/layout accessibility | `accessibility_tests`, `guided_play::tests::browser_and_owned_panel_surface_expose_every_pointer_control`, keyboard-focus tests, reduced-motion tests, and `ui_layout` tests | Automated ownership, scale bounds, keyboard commands, pointer controls, and reduced-motion paths pass. Human usability remains unverified. |
| Regression isolation | `./scripts/check.sh` | Competitive scenario hashes, golden replays, setup defaults, protocol/server behavior, and full workspace tests pass. |

The archived exact challenge branching factors are 24 (mate), 13 (capture), 16
(terrain route), 14 (settlement defense), 5 (production), and 4
(underpromotion). Every exact challenge has a one-action shortest solution; the
production challenge accepts five alternatives. Four of six require terrain,
settlement, production, promotion, or realm-control knowledge.

On the six-piece Warden challenge position after South claims `(2, 4)`, the
node-limited Warden search reproducibly completes depth 4 with 508 ordinary
nodes, 1,157 quiescence nodes, 852 cutoffs, and a depth-limit stop. These are
solver workload measurements, not a human difficulty rating.

## Human evidence still required

No representative new-player or experienced-player guided sessions have been
run for this record. Consequently there is no evidence yet for delayed recall,
explanation in the player's own words, perceived difficulty ordering,
misconceptions, time-to-completion, hint dependence, or usability at every
supported scale/orientation. Do not relabel a difficulty band from the automated
metrics alone.

Use the companion [guided session protocol](guided-session-protocol.md). Keep
the resulting consented, non-identifying records local until deliberately added
to this directory. Task 14.02.05 and the guided-content release gate remain open
until those sessions support the acceptance criteria or drive documented
content revisions.
